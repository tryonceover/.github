# OnceOver

OnceOver finds flights by showing the whole field of dates at once: every pair of departure and return dates in one table, each with its price under your own conditions (stops, connection time, airlines, sellers). One search instead of sixteen tabs, one date each.

The cheapest pair stands out, and every price shows how old it is. Conditions apply on the spot, without a new search. When a trip is cheaper as two separate tickets, the screen says so and gives each ticket its own link.
